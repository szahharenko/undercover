# What is Undercover?
Undercover is a coworking space designed for productivity and collaboration.

# Where is Undercover located?
We are located in the heart of the city, easily accessible by public transport.

# What are the operating hours?
We are open Monday to Friday, from 9 AM to 6 PM.

# Do you offer day passes?
Yes, we offer flexible day passes as well as monthly memberships.

# How can I book a meeting room?
Meeting rooms can be booked through our online portal after you become a member.
